# 00 — VISION: why POXY WORLD exists

POXY WORLD is a digital atelier of collectible figures, built by AETRYX LTD (UK).
Players open cases, collect limited-run figures across seasons, display them in
a personal Museum, trade on a player Market, and climb a Collector Score
leaderboard. Season 1 is "Japanese Memories": 24 figures with public run sizes.

## The problem with the category

Gacha and lootbox products are, in most people's eyes, casinos with cartoons.
Odds are hidden or misrepresented, "limited" items are quietly reprinted,
duplicates are pure loss, and the whole experience is engineered noise:
sirens, confetti, fake urgency. People play them and feel bad about it.

## Our answer: proof, not promises

Honesty is not a compliance checkbox here; it is the product's competitive
advantage and its single brand pillar.

- **Public odds.** Every case shows its exact drop rates before purchase.
  Odds live in the database, are shown in the UI, and are published on a
  printable public page.
- **Verifiable openings.** Every opening uses commit-reveal RNG: the server
  commits to a hashed seed before you roll, and reveals it after. A public
  verify page recomputes the result in your browser. You do not have to
  trust us; you can check.
- **Real scarcity.** Every figure has a published run size. When a run is
  minted out, it is gone. Sold-out states and remaining counts are public
  and live.

## The feeling

A museum, not a fair. The interface is calm, cold, precise; the figure is the
only thing that glows. Opening a case is a ritual, not a slot machine: the
lights go down, the seal is shown, the figure forms. Owning is the point —
the Museum, the Passport, the Collector Score make a collection feel like
something you curated, not something you gambled into.

## Who it is for

Adults (18+) who collect: people who care about editions, provenance and
display, who trade deliberately, and who reward brands that treat them as
intelligent. Not whales hunted by dark patterns — the design includes
spending limits and self-exclusion from day one, by conviction and by UK
compliance posture.

## Where it goes

Season by season: new figure sets with finite runs, a deepening collector
culture (sets, atlases, leaderboards, showcases), player-to-player economy
with real sinks, and — after the beta proves the loop — richer figure
presentation (Rive animation) and partner drops. The long game is to be the
place where digital collecting is provably fair and quietly beautiful.
